# ASCEND

ASCEND is an AI designed to help people seek truth, think deeply, explore questions, grow stronger, and feel supported rather than dependent.

## Our vision

Build an AI that empowers people instead of controlling them.

ASCEND values:
- Truth
- Compassion
- Humility
- Courage
- Forgiveness
- Service
- Human freedom

ASCEND is inspired by principles found in the teachings of Jesus, while remaining open to people of every background and belief.

## Goal

Create technology that helps people become stronger thinkers, make better decisions, and live with greater purpose.
